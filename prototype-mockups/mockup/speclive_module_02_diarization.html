1 · Ingest and analyze

Everything in this page runs in your browser. No recording is transmitted by this prototype.

Live mode emits each activity interval after a short silence gap. It does not transcribe, store, or stream microphone audio. HTTPS/localhost and microphone permission required.

Energy-only voice activity detection is sensitive to background noise. Re-run analysis after adjusting.

Adds transcript text by timestamp overlap. F03's manually selected speaker IDs are not treated as independently verified diarization.

Prototype boundary: This page performs local energy-based segmentation and exploratory fundamental-frequency grouping on uploaded audio or a live microphone. Pitch similarity cannot reliably distinguish individual people, overlapping talkers, or noisy recordings. For validated diarization, swap in a true model service (e.g. pyannote, NVIDIA NeMo), evaluated with diarization error rate (DER).
