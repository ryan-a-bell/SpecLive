Requirements lifecycle · from machine proposal to authority

Candidate / derived: explicit or inferred with evidence and declared reasoning basis.
Ambiguous / potentially conflicting: requires a discriminating question, not silent resolution.
Proposed reconciliation: a new conversation statement may narrow interpretation, supersede previous claims, or leave conflict unresolved.
Validation queue: F06 keeps coherent synthesized candidates, linked to exact original sources; uncertain items remain open.
Human validated: F08 approval establishes what becomes authoritative.
A conflict between a target and current performance is a feasibility gap; two incompatible requested behaviors form a potential requirements conflict. The system should represent both without conflating them.
